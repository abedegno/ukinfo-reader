About the project ↗

Add an edition

Choose a folder containing extracted UK-Info database files. The app identifies the edition and checks its source fingerprints.

Complete editions can take tens of minutes and require several gigabytes of free disk space. Samples follow source order; a small 2005 sample may contain no businesses.

Open a saved record or address

Paste a saved link to open its source record or address in this catalogue. This also works with links saved from another session or installation.
